About Us

关于我们

We are interested in many frontier topics at the intersection of nano-device engineering and photonic physics. We believe the essence of research is pure exploration and creation, and we are therefore committed to maintaining an equal, inclusive, and mutually supportive team environment. We hope every member can take pure delight in exploring the unknown, unleash creativity in an open and free atmosphere, and eventually grow into an independent and competent researcher. If you are interested in our research, feel free to contact the PI directly at majw@sustech.edu.cn. You will typically receive a reply within 3 business days.
我们对纳米器件工程与光子物理交叉领域的许多前沿课题都感兴趣。我们相信科研的本质是纯粹的探索与创造，因此我们致力于维持一个平等包容互助的团队环境。我们希望让每一位成员都能纯粹地享受探索未知的乐趣，在开放自由的氛围中释放创造力，最终成长为独当一面的科研工作者。如果你对我们的研究感兴趣，欢迎直接联系PI: majw@sustech.edu.cn，你一般会在3 个工作日内收到回复。

Postdoctoral Fellow
博士后研究员
We currently have 2-3 openings for postdoctoral fellows in the areas of integrated photonics, quantum optics, and meta-materials.

Collaboration Philosophy:
We'll work together to develop research topics that fit your background, give you the resources you need, and push the field forward. We support grant applications, conference attendance, and career development.

Basic Requirements:
• Ph.D. degree from top-tier universities (985/211 institutions or internationally prestigious universities)
• Applicants must have first-author publications in related research areas, demonstrating the capability and potential to conduct high-quality research.

Salaries & Benefits:
• Annual salary: 350,000–450,000 RMB (including Shenzhen postdoc subsidy)
• Comprehensive social insurance (medical, pension, work injury insurance, etc.)
• Housing subsidy and well-equipped research facilities

Master/Ph.D. Student
硕士/博士研究生
We always welcome motivated students with genuine curiosity in photonics and physics. For details, please refer to the University Admissions Website.

Mentoring Philosophy:
New members get hands-on training from senior lab members and the PI, then gradually move into independent projects. You pick your research direction based on what interests you. We'll work together on research ideas and guidance, exploring scientific frontiers and publishing high-impact results.

Basic Requirements:
• Bachelor's or Master's degree in optoelectronics, electronic engineering, physics, or related disciplines.
• Strong academic background (GPA ≥ 85/100 or 3.5/4.0) from top-tier universities.

Preferred Expertise:
• Device nanofabrication / material synthesis
• Device design / numerical simulations / theoretical modeling
• Chip / fiber / free-space optoelectronic characterization
• Artificial intelligence algorithms / programming
我们持续招收对光子学的工程和物理有浓厚兴趣的研究生。详情请参考学校官网。
